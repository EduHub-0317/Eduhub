What Is a PYQ Paper, and How Is It Different From a Model Paper?

PYQ stands for Previous Year Questions. A PYQ paper is the actual question paper students sat in a past exam. A model paper (also called a sample paper) is written afterwards, by teachers or the board, to show the pattern. Nobody sat it as a real exam. That difference changes how you should use each one:
  • PYQ papers show you what was really asked, how hard it was, and which chapters came up again and again.
  • Model papers are fresh practice. Use them after you've finished the PYQs, when you want questions you haven't seen.
    • The confusion is real. Some websites list model papers under titles like "2025 question paper," so a student downloads a sample thinking it's the real exam. Before you trust any file, check two things: whether the title says "model" or "sample," and whether the exam name and year are printed at the top of the paper itself.

    Five Mistakes Students Make With Old Papers

  • Reading the answers before trying. It feels like studying, but you only learn that the answer looks familiar. Try first, then check.
  • Skipping the timer. An untimed paper hides your real problem. Many students know the content and still run out of time on the long answers.
  • Practising only the favourite subject. Maths students tend to redo Maths. The subject you avoid is the one with the most marks to gain.
  • Writing only the final answer. In Maths and Science, the steps are where marks live. Write them even when you can do the sum in your head.

    Is the 2025 Paper Still Useful for the New Syllabus?

    Yes, as practice, with one check first. A 2026-27 Class 8 syllabus is now listed by Jharkhand education sites, so some chapters may have been added, removed or reorganized since 2025. Use the 2025 paper like this:

    1. Open the latest JAC Class 8 syllabus and put it next to your 2025 chapter table.

    2. Chapters in both: use the 2025 questions fully. They're still on target.

    3. Chapters dropped from the new syllabus: skip those questions.

    4. New chapters: the 2025 paper can't help here. Use the JAC Class 8 model paper and the end-of-chapter exercises in your textbook.

    The pattern of the paper, meaning the sections, the question types and the time pressure, is useful even when the chapter list changes. Confirm the syllabus on the official JAC website before you plan your revision.
